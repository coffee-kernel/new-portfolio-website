What this policy covers

This policy explains how Jose Joshua Quintanar handles information submitted through this portfolio website. This site is a personal portfolio and does not sell personal information or use information for advertising profiles.

Information you provide

If you use the contact form, the site receives your name, email address, message, and any information you choose to include. Please do not send passwords, payment details, sensitive personal information, or confidential business information through the form.

Information used for security

The contact endpoint may process your IP address or related request information temporarily to limit abuse and protect the form. This information is used for security and rate limiting, not for advertising.

How information is used

  • To read and respond to your inquiry.
  • To operate, secure, and troubleshoot the website.
  • To prevent spam, fraud, and abusive requests.

Service providers

The website is hosted through Vercel. If email delivery is enabled in the deployment, submitted messages may be sent through Resend. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts. These providers may process technical information under their own policies and terms.

Retention and deletion

Messages are retained only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain business records, resolve disputes, or protect the website. Delivery-provider and hosting logs may have their own retention periods. To request deletion of a message you submitted, email joshuaquintanar17@gmail.com.

Your choices

You may choose not to submit the contact form. You can request access, correction, or deletion of information you sent through the form, subject to applicable law and legitimate record-keeping needs.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the website or its services change. The date above indicates when it was last revised.
