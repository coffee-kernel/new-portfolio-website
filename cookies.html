Cookies & Local Storage Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

No advertising or tracking cookies

This portfolio does not currently use advertising cookies, analytics cookies, social-media pixels, or cross-site tracking technologies. No cookie consent banner is required for the current site behavior.

Theme preference

When you switch between dark and light mode, the site stores your preference in your browser's local storage under portfolio-theme. This is a strictly functional preference. It is not sent to the site and is not used to identify or track you.

Third-party requests

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser may share technical request information with Google when downloading those fonts. Hosting and contact-form services may also process technical request information as described in the Privacy Policy.

Managing local storage

You can clear local storage through your browser's site data or privacy settings. Clearing it will reset the theme preference; it will not affect the rest of the website.

Future changes

If analytics, advertising, embedded media, or other non-essential tracking is added, this policy will be updated and any required consent choice will be provided before those technologies are used.
