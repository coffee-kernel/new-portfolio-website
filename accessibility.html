Accessibility Statement

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Our commitment

Jose Joshua Quintanar aims to make this portfolio usable by as many people as possible, including people who use keyboard navigation, screen readers, zoom, or other assistive technologies.

Current measures

  • Semantic page structure and labeled navigation landmarks.
  • Visible focus and keyboard-friendly controls.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful controls and decorative treatment for visual effects.
  • Responsive layouts and support for reduced-motion preferences where practical.

Feedback

Accessibility is an ongoing process. If you encounter a barrier, please email joshuaquintanar17@gmail.com with the page, the problem, and the assistive technology or browser you used if relevant.

Limitations

Some third-party websites linked from this portfolio may not be under our control. We will consider reasonable improvements to this site when accessibility issues are reported.
